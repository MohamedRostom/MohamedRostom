About

Engineer by craft.
Automator by conviction.

I'm a Test Automation Engineer specializing in automotive embedded systems, with deep experience designing CI/CD pipelines, Python test frameworks, and HIL environments for ADAS ECU validation.

Currently embedded on-site at Mercedes-Benz as a Resident Engineer via Luxoft, where I work directly with OEM engineers to automate the validation of safety-critical ADAS functions — from sensor fusion to driver assistance features.

Before that, I spent two years at Valeo building embedded Linux software and automation pipelines for Nissan and Volkswagen telematics ECUs.

I believe the engineer's job in the AI era isn't to write every line of code — it's to own the problem, set the direction, and ensure quality at every layer of the system.

Engineering Philosophy

How I think about
building software.

💡
Ideas drive everything
Every system starts with a problem worth solving. The clearest ideas — about what to build, what to skip, and what failure looks like — produce the best software.
🤖
AI amplifies, engineers judge
AI can generate any algorithm. The engineer's job is to review it for correctness, security, and fit to the use case. Domain knowledge is what separates a good result from a dangerous one.
⚖️
Everything has a cost
Time, money, resources, human power. Engineers set priorities based on inputs and work accordingly. Knowing what NOT to build is as valuable as knowing how to build.
